Range Trader — simulation

One line primitive, four independent flags (Buy Entry / Sell Entry / Buy Exit / Sell Exit), each configurable per line. Mode just filters which flags are shown. Lines re-trigger every touch until playback passes their own second anchor point — then they go dead (greyed).

Click the chart to place a line's 1st point, click again for the 2nd (further right = stays armed longer). Switching mode reloads a fresh preset for that mode.

Press SL, drag, release

The line follows the cursor and lands where you let go. It takes the whole position by default.

2

Fully covered: the SL button hides

10 lots held, 10 lots under SL. Nothing is left to protect, so the button goes.

3

Cut the SL to 5 lots: the button is back

Click the quantity on the SL line and type 5. The other 5 lots have no SL, so SL reappears.

4

A second SL takes the left quantity

Press SL again and drop it lower. It covers the remaining 5 lots, and the button hides again.

5

Targets follow the same rule

The first TP takes all 10 lots by default.

6

Split the target: a second TP for the other 5

Cut the first TP to 5 lots, press TP again, and place the second one higher.

7

Two SLs and two TPs on one position

Each covers a part of the 10 lots. Nothing is covered twice.

8

A partial fill: exits follow the position

The first SL sold 5 lots, so 5 are held. Targets were set for 10, so the farthest one is dropped.

9

The position closes: leftover exits go

The remaining target fills for the last 5 lots, and the other SL is cancelled with the position.

1

A strategy spans hundreds of points

On a 1m chart the screen holds about 80 points. Switch to 1h or 4h so every strike and the whole payoff fit.

2

Start a basket and pick the first leg

Hover a strike on the index chart and press −CE. The payoff appears on the chart straight away.

3

Scroll the price scale for more strikes

Drag the right-hand price scale to bring the next strike into view, then choose +CE to cap the risk.

4

Add the put side, one leg at a time

Scroll down and choose −PE, then +PE. Each leg reshapes the payoff, and the banner keeps max profit, max loss and breakevens up to date.

5

Read the whole payoff at a glance

Green where the strategy makes money, red where it loses, dashed lines at the breakevens. It only reads well because 1h shows the full range.

6

Place it as one multi-leg order

Enter places all four legs together. No other screen for strategy orders.

7

Set a rupee SL on the whole basket

One limit for the strategy.

8

The limit is hit: every leg exits

All four legs close at market, and the basket shows what was realised.
